Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Some quick answers before your pet’s next grooming visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Choose your preferred service and use the Book Appointment or Contact button to reach the grooming team. Your booking is confirmed once the team responds.

What services can I choose from?

FurCast lists Pet Grooming, Nail Trimming, Pet Bath, Extra Haircut, Ear Cleaning, and Other Pet Care Services.

Can I request an extra haircut or detangling?

Yes. Mention the add-on you want when you contact the grooming team so they can confirm availability and any additional cost.

How long will grooming take?

Timing depends on your pet’s size, coat condition, selected service, and comfort needs. Ask the grooming team when requesting your appointment.

Is online booking active on this demo website?

This is a landing-page concept. Appointment requests are not saved automatically, so please use the contact options to discuss a booking.
